# Merchant Center Help Community Draft

**Status:** Draft only. Do not post without the owner’s separate approval, because a Help Community question is public.

## Suggested title

Need specific guidance on persistent Merchant Center Misrepresentation after two reviews

## Suggested post

>Hello,
>
>I am seeking specific guidance for a Canadian Merchant Center account that remains blocked for an account-level **Misrepresentation** issue after two unsuccessful reviews. We do not want to submit another blind review.
>
>The store uses an existing Shopify Google & YouTube product connection and an already verified and claimed website domain. Before the two reviews, we completed a transparency audit and made factual alignment updates: public About, Contact, Shipping, Returns, Privacy, and Terms pages are available in the footer; the business name, website, and customer-service email are aligned in Merchant Center; Canadian shipping and defective-only return terms are publicly disclosed; checkout works; and product landing pages use the verified domain.
>
>The account is still blocked in Canada for Misrepresentation, but the diagnostic does not identify a specific remaining page, product, or identity-verification task. The Merchant Center support form currently requires a Google Ads Customer ID even though this account has no linked Google Ads account or campaign, so we cannot submit that form truthfully.
>
>Could a Google representative or experienced product expert clarify the correct official path to obtain a specific remaining signal or an identity-verification prompt for an account that has already completed two reviews? We can provide non-sensitive screenshots through an official private workflow if needed. Thank you.

## Do not include in a public post

| Do not post | Reason |
|---|---|
| Private legal residence or customer correspondence address | Unnecessary personal-location disclosure. |
| Government identity documents, payment-profile details, or account-security material | Community posts are public and cannot be used as an identity-verification channel. |
| Full Merchant Center account ID, Google-account email, Google Ads identifiers, or checkout tokens | Not needed to explain the issue and should be minimized. |
| Customer reviews, certifications, partnership claims, or product promises that are not independently verifiable | These could create further policy or consumer-protection risk. |

## Supporting official context

Google says merchants uncertain about an issue or how to proceed may contact support, and it presents its Help Community as a distinct next step. Its review guidance also says to complete identity verification when prompted in the **Needs attention** tab and warns that reviews should follow a thorough policy audit.[1]
