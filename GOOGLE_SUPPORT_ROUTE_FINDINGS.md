# Google Merchant Center Support-Route Findings

**Date:** August 20, 2026  
**Account:** Nestwell — Merchant Center `5820758319`  
**Scope:** Identify a factual support route for the repeated Misrepresentation rejection without creating, linking, or inventing a Google Ads account.

## Conclusion

Google’s published Merchant Center guidance points merchants who are uncertain about an account-suspension issue to the same **suspended-account contact form** at `https://support.google.com/merchants/contact/suspended`. Google’s suspension guidance also points Shopify users to the Google & YouTube app’s support path, while the installed Shopify Google & YouTube channel directs a suspended merchant back to Merchant Center to contact Google support. No official, private, one-to-one Merchant Center route that bypasses the required Google Ads Customer ID field was identified in this review.[1][2]

## Verified Constraints

The Nestwell account has **no linked Google Ads account, campaign, or spend**. The official suspended-account form was configured with the factual Merchant Center account, company, administrator, contact, policy, and website details. It then labels **Google Ads Customer ID** as required. The temporary-account-access option was left at **No** because choosing Yes created a required Google Ads authorization attachment that requested an Ads Customer ID and campaign names. No false ID, campaign, authorization attachment, Ads link, review, or support case was submitted.

> The official review guidance says to contact Merchant Center support if a merchant is uncertain about the issue or how to proceed. It does not document an alternate private support channel for a merchant without an Ads customer ID.[1]

## Safe Next Step

Keep the prepared case **unsubmitted**. Do not create or link Google Ads merely to satisfy a form field, and do not submit another Merchant Center review without a specific Google rationale or an explicit identity-verification prompt. If the owner wants a further escalation attempt, the only clearly documented alternative is a carefully reviewed **public Merchant Center Help Community** question; it must not disclose private legal-address, account-security, or identity-document details.
